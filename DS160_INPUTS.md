# DS-160 input checklist

Use this only as a preparation checklist. The live form changes its questions
based on the visa category and previous answers, so it remains the source of
truth. Answer every question accurately; do not guess or let automation sign or
submit the application.

## Getting started

- Embassy or consulate location
- CAPTCHA (completed manually)
- Application ID generated by CEAC
- Security question and answer used to recover the application

## Personal information

- Surname and given names exactly as shown on the passport
- Full name in the native alphabet
- Other names or aliases used, including maiden, religious, professional, or
  previous legal names
- Telecode name, if applicable
- Sex
- Marital status
- Date of birth
- City, state/province, and country/region of birth
- Current nationality
- Other current or previous nationalities and related passport details
- Permanent-resident status in another country, if any
- National identification number
- U.S. Social Security number and U.S. taxpayer ID, if any

## Address, phone, email, and social media

- Current home street address, city, state/province, postal code, and country
- Mailing address, if different
- Primary, secondary, and work phone numbers, as applicable
- Other phone numbers used during the requested history period
- Current email address and other email addresses used during the requested
  history period
- Social-media platforms and account identifiers/handles requested by the form

## Passport or travel document

- Passport/travel-document type
- Document number and passport book number, if applicable
- Issuing country or authority
- City, state/province, and country of issuance
- Issue and expiration dates
- Details of lost or stolen passports/travel documents, if any

## Trip to the United States

- Purpose of trip and visa category
- Whether specific travel plans have been made
- Intended arrival date and length of stay
- Arrival/departure dates, flights, cities, and locations to be visited when
  plans are specific
- Address where the applicant will stay in the United States
- Person or organization paying for the trip
- Payer's relationship, contact information, and address when someone else pays

## Travel companions

- Whether anyone else is traveling with the applicant
- Whether traveling as part of a group or organization
- Names of accompanying people and relationship to the applicant

## Previous U.S. travel and immigration history

- Dates and lengths of previous U.S. visits
- Previous U.S. driver's-license details, if requested
- Previous U.S. visa issue date and visa number
- Whether applying for the same visa type and at the same location
- Whether fingerprints were taken
- Details of lost, stolen, cancelled, or revoked U.S. visas
- Visa refusals, refused admissions, or withdrawn entry applications
- ESTA denial, if applicable
- Immigrant petitions filed for the applicant, including who filed them

## U.S. contact

- Contact person's surname and given name, or organization name
- Relationship to the applicant
- U.S. street address
- Phone number
- Email address

## Family information

- Father's full name, date of birth, and whether he is in the United States
- Mother's full name, date of birth, and whether she is in the United States
- Immediate relatives in the United States and their immigration status
- Other relatives in the United States
- Current spouse's full name, date and place of birth, nationality, and address
- Previous spouse details, when applicable
- Fiancé/fiancée details for K visa applications, when applicable

## Work, education, and training

- Primary occupation
- Current employer or school name and full address
- Start date, monthly income where requested, and description of duties
- Previous employers: names, addresses, phone numbers, supervisors, job titles,
  employment dates, and duties
- Schools attended at secondary level or above: names, addresses, courses of
  study, and attendance dates
- Clan or tribe, if applicable
- Languages spoken
- Countries/regions visited during the requested history period
- Professional, social, or charitable organizations
- Specialized skills or training, including firearms, explosives, nuclear,
  biological, or chemical experience
- Military service: country, branch, rank, specialty, and service dates
- Paramilitary, vigilante, rebel, guerrilla, or insurgent involvement

## Security and background

The form asks yes/no questions and requires explanations for applicable “yes”
answers. Topics include:

- Communicable diseases of public-health significance
- Physical or mental conditions that may pose a threat
- Drug abuse or addiction
- Arrests, convictions, controlled-substance violations, prostitution,
  commercialized vice, money laundering, and human trafficking
- Espionage, sabotage, export-control violations, terrorism, support for
  terrorist organizations, genocide, torture, extrajudicial killing, political
  violence, child-soldier recruitment, and severe religious-freedom violations
- Immigration fraud or misrepresentation, removals/deportations, unlawful
  presence, withholding custody of a U.S. citizen child, unlawful voting, and
  renouncing U.S. citizenship to avoid taxation

## Visa-specific information

Depending on the selected visa class, CEAC may also request:

- Student/exchange visitor: SEVIS ID, school/program name and address, course of
  study, and additional contacts
- Petition-based worker: petition receipt number, petitioner/employer details,
  job title, duties, work location, and salary
- Crew/transit: vessel or airline and transit details
- Temporary business: employer and U.S. business/contact details
- K visa: petitioner, relationship, and prior marriage information

## Photo, assistance, review, and signature

- A compliant digital visa photo, where online upload is required
- Name and organization of anyone who helped prepare the application
- Applicant's confirmation that all answers were reviewed
- Passport/travel-document number and final electronic signature
- Final CAPTCHA and submission, completed personally by the applicant
- Confirmation-page barcode saved or printed after submission

## Privacy and preparation notes

- Do not put real applicant data in source-controlled files.
- Store sensitive data outside the repository and exclude it from logs and
  screenshots.
- Keep the Application ID and security answer securely.
- CEAC can time out after 20 minutes of inactivity; save frequently.
- The applicant must personally review, electronically sign, and submit.
